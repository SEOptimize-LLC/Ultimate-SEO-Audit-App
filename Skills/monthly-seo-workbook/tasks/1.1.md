## Period

**Last complete calendar month vs the month before.** Not rolling 30 days: the
client's report is calendar-month, and rolling windows will not reconcile with it.

If the current month is not over, you are reporting the previous one. Running on
2026-08-14 means July vs June.

**Snapshot date (B4) is the date you ran it**, not the period end. It answers
"how fresh is this", which is its whole job.

## Inputs

| Cell group | Source | Call |
|---|---|---|
| Clicks, impressions | GSC | `get_advanced_search_analytics`, `dimensions=date`, sum the rows |
| Branded impressions | GSC | same, plus `filter_dimension=query`, `filter_operator=contains`, `filter_expression=<brand token>` |
| Top landing pages | GSC | same, `dimensions=page`, `row_limit=45` minimum |
| Organic conversions | GA4 | `run_report`, `dimensions=["sessionDefaultChannelGroup"]`, `metrics=["sessions","conversions"]`, both date ranges in one call |
| AI Assistant channel | GA4 | same call, read the `AI Assistant` row |
| Position Tracking | SEMrush | `campaigns` for the project, then `tracking_overview_organic` per campaign |

`run_report` **requires** a `dimensions` value; it errors without one. Passing
both date ranges in a single call returns `date_range_0` (current) and
`date_range_1` (prior), which halves the calls.

## Derivation rules

These exist because each one was got wrong once. Do not re-derive them.

### Consolidate URL variants before ranking pages

Pull **45 or more** page rows, never 10. Strip everything from `?` onward, group
by path, sum clicks, *then* take the top 10.

Taking GSC's raw top 10 gives four homepage rows and hides real pages. On
Superior the homepage appears under **8 URLs** (bare plus 7 UTM/GBP-tagged) and
reads 180 clicks instead of 373.

This is a reporting artifact, **not a site fault**: the tagged URLs
self-canonicalise to the bare homepage (verified 2026-08-14). Do not recommend
removing the UTMs; they carry GA4 attribution. Note it and move on.

### Resolve redirects and sum under the destination

**If a URL 301s, it and its destination are the same page.** Fetch the top ~25
candidate URLs, follow redirects, group by the final 200 URL, and sum. Apply the
same mapping to both periods, or the month-over-month comparison comes from two
different groupings.

Owner's rule, and it matters more than it sounds. GSC keeps reporting the old
URL for weeks after a migration, so an un-merged page reads as two weaker pages
and can miss the top 10 entirely.

Resolve this **live**, never by URL shape. An earlier version pattern-matched
`/x/` against `/blog/x/`, which caught one migration style and missed every
other redirect. Live resolution across the eight clients found **21 redirecting
URLs**, only two of which were the blog case:

| Client | Redirecting URLs found | Example |
|---|---|---|
| SameDay | 11 | `/careers/` -> `/about-us/careers/` (20 clicks) |
| Beehive | 6 | `/park-city-utah/` -> `/our-locations/park-city/` (51 clicks) |
| Superior | 2 | legacy blog URL, and an ice-maker page move |
| Comfort | 2 | `/fireplace-repair-salt-lake-city/` -> a service-area URL |

SameDay's eleven are residue from its June 2026 permalink restructure. Without
merging, its whole section C would have double-counted.

An unreachable URL is left standing on its own rather than guessed at.

**Before flagging a redirect as a problem, check it is one.** On Superior the
legacy blog URL already 301'd and canonicalised correctly, so the right note was
"no action" rather than the fix first proposed.

### Impressions YoY inside the bug window

Any comparison touching **May 2025 to April 2026** has inflated impressions.
Leave the impressions YoY cell **blank** and say why in Notes. Clicks are
unaffected and remain valid.

The signature, if you want to confirm: Superior's July 2025 shows 3.6x the
impressions at average position 45-58 against 17-27 now. Inflated
low-position impressions, exactly as documented.

This rule expires with the **April 2027** report, when the prior-year period
finally clears the window.

### Position Tracking: every campaign, not just one

Clients have several campaigns at different geographic scopes. **Write one row
per campaign**, ordered city, then state, then country.

Use `tracking_overview_organic` (100 API units flat). Do **not** use
`tracking_position_organic`, which bills 100 units *per keyword*.

**`total` and `all` are two different populations. Do not mix them in one row
without reconciling.**

- `total` = keywords in the campaign (the `Keywords Tracked` column)
- `all` = keywords ranking **in the top 100** (equals `top100`)
- `all_improved` / `all_declined` describe only `all`
- Flat = `all` minus `all_improved` minus `all_declined`
- **Not ranking = `total` minus `all`**, and this is the bucket that makes the
  row add up

Improved + Declined + Flat sums to `all`, never to `total`. On Superior that is
22 + 9 + 8 = 39 against 46 tracked, and a reader checking the arithmetic finds
seven keywords missing. **The Notes cell must open with the reconciliation**, in
the form `22 + 9 + 8 + 7 not-ranking = 46`.

The not-ranking count is a finding, not an accounting nuisance. Utah shows 16 of
38 tracked keywords ranking nowhere in the top 100, which is gap material for 1.5
and 1.7. Say so in Notes.

> **Template improvement worth making:** section B has no `Not Ranking` column,
> which is why this has to live in prose. Adding one between `Flat` and `Notes`
> would make the row self-evidently correct. Not done unilaterally, as it changes
> the owner's template for all eight clients.

Section B has only **three rows** before the section C header at row 22. Assert
row 22 still begins with `C.` before writing. A client with four or more
campaigns needs rows inserted first.

A national campaign for a local business will drift down and that is correct,
not a problem to solve. Say so rather than leaving a red number.

### Registry flags

`ecommerce: false` means the GA4 revenue row is `N/A`, not blank and not zero.
`is_local: false` means Position Tracking may have only one campaign.

`Metric | This Period | Prior Period | YoY (if clean) | Notes`.

**Number formats, applied every run, not on request:**

- `B8:D15`, `B19:E21`, `B24:C33` -> `FMT_INT` (`#,##0`)
- `D24:D33` -> `FMT_DELTA` (`+#,##0;-#,##0;0`)

Apply to the whole range, not just filled cells, so later rows inherit it.

**Then seed 4.2:** write `='1.1 Data Snapshot'!A24` through `A28` into
`'4.2 Conversion Paths'!A5:A9`. **Five, not ten** — 4.2's own subtitle says "Top
5 landers from 1.1 section C". Guard 4.2's header row first.
