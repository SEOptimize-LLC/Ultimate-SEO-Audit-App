## Preflight: enforce the union, not the wiring

The task text requires reviewing 1.1-1.7; ClickUp gates only 1.1/1.5/1.6/1.7.
This system enforces the union (per the design spec): every input's

## Stage 0 - the quick-win scan (section A)

`scan_title_gaps.py` runs the ClickUp quick-win test across the WHOLE site, not
the 1.5 shortlist: pages ranking positions 4-20 for queries that appear in
neither the title nor the meta, ranked by the impressions on those missing
queries. The test and its thresholds are in [2.3.md](2.3.md). Every page that
fails a filter stays in the output with its reason (brand, avg-pos illusion, or
cooldown) so the "Excluded?" column is where the owner checks the call.

`--write` stores `Clients/<folder>/monthly_plan/quick_win_scan.json`, which is
the ONLY source of section A rows and of quick-win pages. `fill_1_8.py` refuses
a quick win the scan did not find, and one the scan excluded.

## Stage 1 - the matrix (plan_1_8.py)

One evidence table per client joining the three signals: 1.5 candidates
(read off the tab), 1.7 clusters (analysis.yaml; parked rows excluded), and
1.6 AI-visibility gaps (category prompts absent on >= 2 platforms, attached
by query-word overlap). The template's own rule ranks it: a page holding all
three signals outranks any single-purpose target.

Two caveats the pilot taught:

- **"Unmatched 1.6 gaps" means unmatched against the CANDIDATES, not the
  site.** EW's OTES and NY prompts looked like new-page ideas; both pages
  existed. Verify existence before routing anything as a new build.
- **The matrix is a shortlist generator, not a verdict.** Present selections
  as "capacity-filled from N ranked candidates", never as "only these are
  worth doing" - the owner's quick GSC scan found real decay the shortlist
  framing had hidden.

## Stage 2 - the judgment gates (all four, in order)

1. **Year-over-year control** on every proposed target - same-window YoY
   clicks, not sequential. Full rule and both failure directions in
   [1.5.md](1.5.md) ("Year-over-year control is MANDATORY"). Sequential-only
   reads hid an -86% collapse on a summer-peak page and would over-flag
   school-year content every June.
2. **Last-updated gate** (owner rule, 2026-08-19): check every target's
   update date BEFORE locking - visible date, JSON-LD dateModified,
   article:modified_time, or the CMS when the site blocks reads (EW). A page
   updated inside the recrawl window (~4 weeks) gets one of: (a) pulled to
   measurement when the update already implements the brief (EW's T-TESS and
   Marzano - verify by parsing the live page against the brief's asks);
   (b) kept with a step-0 revision-diff instruction when the gap is additive
   or the update coincides with a crash (EW's Danielson); (c) kept when the
   window has passed without recovery (4+ weeks = the update is judged
   insufficient). Re-touching day-old work without checking is how agency
   effort gets paid twice - Comfort's furnace page was edited the day before
   its brief shipped.
3. **Selection**: up to 5 refresh targets (from 1.5 and the matrix) and up to 5
   quick wins (from the scan), 10 pages at most. Commercial routing and click
   viability per [1.7.md](1.7.md), scope rule enforced - anything needing
   substantial new copy routes to the Content list (SameDay's AC-install page,
   Comfort's city builds, Cudio's OpenERP container), it never takes a refresh
   slot. An honest 3 beats a padded 5 (Arsenal), and an empty quick-win half is
   a valid finding. Owner confirms. Each URL gets ONE title-and-meta
   recommendation a month, so a page cannot be both a refresh target and a quick
   win.
4. **Content-calendar cross-check**: the Y/N column in section B belongs to
   the CONTENT MANAGER, not us (owner delegation, 2026-08-19). Pre-fill the
   candidate pages, leave B/C blank, and note the pending check in the
   summary. Standing decision for any Y: "Sequence: content first - SEO next
   month".

## Stage 3 - populate (fill_1_8.py)

plan.yaml per client: `targets` (refresh pages: url / queries / pos / ai /
angle), `quick_wins` (url, optional note; each must be a non-excluded scan row),
`deep_work` (section C rows: need / source / task), `summary` (exactly 3
bullets for section D).

What it writes:

- **1.8 section A** from the scan file: page, missing queries, position range,
  impressions on the missing queries, the exclusion reason, and Y/N for
  "Selected -> 2.3 Pages". Rows are inserted when the scan holds more than the
  template's five; nothing below is overwritten.
- **2.3 Pages, columns A:E** (Type, Page, URL, Target Queries / Refresh Angle,
  Baseline). Columns F-I (Position Tracking tag, Title/Meta Change, Date Live,
  Verdict) are completion records and are never written here; a re-run refuses
  to touch a row once any of them is filled unless `--force`.
- **Section B** pre-filled with the page URLs; the Y/N belongs to the CONTENT
  MANAGER, not us, and the Decision dropdown is repaired on every run.
- **Sections C and D**, and the 1.5 tab's "Selected in 1.8?" column, trued up
  to the actual lock so the two tabs cannot disagree.
- **Not ClickUp.** 1.8 adds one checklist line per page to the ClickUp 2.3 task;
  the lines are printed for pasting (no write path to ClickUp checklists exists
  here).

Baselines are COMPUTED, never typed: clicks and impressions come from a fresh
Search Console page-level pull over the window 1.5 uses (three whole months
ending last month), so every number traces to a query run today and the two
tabs reconcile. A page with no rows in the window says so rather than
reading as zero.

**Refresh Angle vs 1.7 Expansion Angle** (owner asked; the answer is policy):
the 1.7 column is the deep writer brief for competitor-gap clusters; the angle
in Pages column D is the condensed work instruction, derived from the 1.7 brief
where one exists. Decay/AI-visibility targets with no 1.7 row get a full-depth
angle cell instead - there is no deeper document to point at, and briefs must
rest on collected evidence (winners pulls, YoY data), never inference.

**Dropdown repair**: the template once shipped its sequencing option split in
two by the comma inside a ONE_OF_LIST value; `fill_1_8` rewrites the section B
Decision validation to four clean options on every run.

## What October 2026 added (Bart's, owner-approved 2026-10-08: "This looks good. Really good.")

- **Last month's unfinished work enters the plan as CARRYOVER, not as new work.** Read
  `monthly_plan/carryover-<YYYY-MM>.md` (Table B rows routed to 2.3 or 1.8), re-check each item live
  before using it. **Carried pages compete for refresh slots in the revenue order like every other
  candidate** (owner, 2026-10-12, from November 2026): a winner is labelled "(Oct carryover)" (the
  month it came from) with the angle "Finish October's brief"; a loser waits in section C with its numbers and competes again
  next month. Holding a slot by default put Comfort's Bountiful page (7 visits, 0 leads in 12 months)
  ahead of Sandy (16 leads). `url_cooldown` still exempts unfinished carried pages from the cooldown.
  Items needing a decision (architecture, product line) go to section C, never a refresh slot.
- **Items the owner dropped stay out until their date.** Before using any carryover or pending
  item, run `python scripts/pending_drops.py active <client>`. An item that matches an active drop,
  by its pending-item text or by a URL it names for the same work, is not carried, planned or
  re-flagged. Owner, 2026-10-30: rows he deletes from the Pending Items sheet are held out of the
  November and December workbooks and may return from January 2027. `pending_drops.py sync
  <client>` records his deletions after each review.
- **Present the lock and a numbered decision round BEFORE writing.** The owner signs off; then fill.
- **Gates run on every candidate:** YoY plus sequential clicks (`same window last year` and the
  previous quarter), last-modified date (a bulk same-half-hour date across posts = template change,
  keep with a step-0 revision check), live H2/title/meta re-read, and a winners + PAA pull for any
  decay target with no 1.7 brief.
- **Section A carries "US Search Volume on Missing Queries (/mo)"** after the impressions column
  (owner, 2026-10-08), summed from Google Ads volume in `scan_title_gaps.py`; per-query volume
  shows in the missing-queries bullets. `fill_1_8.py` inserts the column on first run.
- **"Selected -> 2.3 Pages" is Y for refresh AND quick-win pages.**
- **URLs are written at their canonical address:** the resolver follows redirects AND a same-path
  canonical that differs only by the trailing slash (Bart's serves both forms with 200). It reads
  the whole `<head>`: Beehive's canonical sits 302,000 characters in.
- **Pages with no SEO value never enter the scan or 1.5** (owner, 2026-10-08): contact, about,
  thank-you, testimonials, reviews, legal, jobs, account, tag and paginated archives, files.
  `scripts/seo_value.py` matches whole path segments, so a blog post ABOUT thank-you notes stays in.
- **An unshipped item from last month on a page still in cooldown is a section C correction**
  ("TITLE NOT LIVE (ROUTED TO 2.3)"), not a refresh slot and never a cooldown override. Beehive's
  suffix, Cudio's cost title and EW's 30/60/90 title went this way in October.
- **Revenue first (owner, 2026-10-08).** Organic traffic only matters as business. Refresh slots
  fill in this order: (1) core revenue service pages in decline, (2) other service pages, (3) posts
  that feed a core service, (4) other posts. Core services come from the client's revenue data
  (requested from Beehive, SameDay, Superior and Comfort); until it arrives, GA4 leads by landing page
  stand in. No dollar figures until the client supplies job values and close rates, and never
  benchmark figures.
- **Every refresh page carries TREND and BUSINESS CASE** in its 2.3 context cell:
  Search Console misreported impressions and position from May 13, 2025 to April 27, 2026 (see 1.5),
  so the position line reads against May-July 2026, not a year ago; clicks still compare with a year ago.
  `scripts/business_case_1_8.py <client>` writes them from `decline_windows.py` (last 90 days vs the
  90 before and vs last year, last 6 months vs the 6 before, the quarter vs last year's) and GA4
  organic visits and leads (`ga4_lead_events` in the registry: phone clicks, submitted forms,
  completed bookings only). Recovery is the page's own level last year, measured, never modelled.
  `fill_1_8.py` merges the blocks. GA4 runs on gcloud ADC; when that has expired, pull the same
  report through the analytics connector into `ga4-pages-<month>.json`.
- **An avg-pos row is a refresh candidate, not a dead row.** A title edit alone will not move a page
  ranking mostly past the top ten; a big gap there belongs in a refresh slot (Beehive's water heater
  installation page, down 83% in clicks, showed as excluded). Section A now says so.
- **The scan skips the cooldown test on rows it already excluded**, so check the work history before
  promoting one. An older row that never shipped still blocks; only the owner overrides
  (`--allow-cooldown <url>`).
- **No SERP evidence, no new decay brief.** When the live SERP endpoint fails, queue the same
  keywords with `/v3/serp/google/organic/task_post` and read them with `task_get/advanced`.
- **Readability is part of done:** written rows are top-aligned with fitted heights, inserted rows
  take the template row's format, and the 2.3 brief column is 420px. `scripts/format_pass.py`
  re-applies this to 1.5-2.3 for any client.

## Gate

Done when: the scan ran and section A shows every row with its exclusion
reason, the 2.3 Pages section carries up to 5 refresh and up to 5 quick-win
pages with computed baselines and evidence-based angles (step-0 notes wherever
the date gate fired), section B lists the candidates for the content manager,
section C routes every decision and new build, section D carries exactly 3
bullets Janelle can paste, the 1.5 flags match the lock, the ClickUp checklist
lines are pasted, and the owner has signed off the selection.
