## Preflight: enforce the union, not the wiring

The task text requires reviewing 1.1-1.7; ClickUp gates only 1.1/1.5/1.6/1.7.
This system enforces the union (per the design spec): every input's
deliverable must exist - tabs with data, files on disk - before selection
runs. The August pilot's step one was in fact a 1.7 remediation pass, which
is what "review the deliverables" means when a review finds them wrong.

## Stage 1 - the matrix (plan_1_8.py)

One evidence table per client joining the three signals: 1.5 candidates
(read off the tab), 1.7 clusters (analysis.yaml; parked rows excluded), and
1.6 AI-visibility gaps (category prompts absent on >= 2 platforms, attached
by query-word overlap). The template's own rule ranks it: a page holding all
three signals outranks any single-purpose target.

Two caveats the pilot taught:

- **"Unmatched 1.6 gaps" means unmatched against the CANDIDATES, not the
  site.** EW's OTES and NY prompts looked like new-page ideas; both pages
  existed. Verify existence before routing anything as a new build.
- **The matrix is a shortlist generator, not a verdict.** Present selections
  as "capacity-filled from N ranked candidates", never as "only these are
  worth doing" - the owner's quick GSC scan found real decay the shortlist
  framing had hidden.

## Stage 2 - the judgment gates (all four, in order)

1. **Year-over-year control** on every proposed target - same-window YoY
   clicks, not sequential. Full rule and both failure directions in
   [1.5.md](1.5.md) ("Year-over-year control is MANDATORY"). Sequential-only
   reads hid an -86% collapse on a summer-peak page and would over-flag
   school-year content every June.
2. **Last-updated gate** (owner rule, 2026-08-19): check every target's
   update date BEFORE locking - visible date, JSON-LD dateModified,
   article:modified_time, or the CMS when the site blocks reads (EW). A page
   updated inside the recrawl window (~4 weeks) gets one of: (a) pulled to
   measurement when the update already implements the brief (EW's T-TESS and
   Marzano - verify by parsing the live page against the brief's asks);
   (b) kept with a step-0 revision-diff instruction when the gap is additive
   or the update coincides with a crash (EW's Danielson); (c) kept when the
   window has passed without recovery (4+ weeks = the update is judged
   insufficient). Re-touching day-old work without checking is how agency
   effort gets paid twice - Comfort's furnace page was edited the day before
   its brief shipped.
3. **Selection**: 3-5 targets, commercial routing and click viability per
   [1.7.md](1.7.md), scope rule enforced - anything needing substantial new
   copy routes to the Content list (SameDay's AC-install page, Comfort's
   city builds, Cudio's OpenERP container), it never takes a refresh slot.
   An honest 3 beats a padded 5 (Arsenal). Owner confirms.
4. **Content-calendar cross-check**: the Y/N column in section B belongs to
   the CONTENT MANAGER, not us (owner delegation, 2026-08-19). Pre-fill the
   candidate pages, leave B/C blank, and note the pending check in the
   summary. Standing decision for any Y: "Sequence: content first - SEO next
   month".

## Stage 3 - populate (fill_1_8.py)

plan.yaml per client: `targets` (url / clickup / queries / pos / ai / angle),
`deep_work`, `summary` (exactly 3 bullets). The filler computes section A
baselines from a fresh GSC May-Jul pull at fill time - clicks and impressions
are never typed by hand - and syncs the 1.5 tab's "Selected in 1.8?" column
to the actual lock, so the two tabs cannot disagree.

**ClickUp mapping** (the dropdown holds exactly 2.3 / 2.4 / 3.1, verified
against the validation rule): highest-value target -> 2.3, second -> 2.4,
targets 3-5 -> 3.1. 2.5 Quick Wins is a list task with its own tab, never a
target slot. Execution logs live in "2.3-3.1 Refresh Log".

**Refresh Angle vs 1.7 Expansion Angle** (owner asked; the answer is policy):
the 1.7 column is the deep writer brief for competitor-gap clusters; the 1.8
angle is the condensed work instruction that travels into the ClickUp task,
derived from the 1.7 brief where one exists. Decay/AI-visibility targets with
no 1.7 row get a full-depth angle cell instead - there is no deeper document
to point at, and briefs must rest on collected evidence (winners pulls, YoY
data), never inference.

**Dropdown repair**: the template shipped its sequencing option split in two
by the comma inside a ONE_OF_LIST value; fill_1_8 rewrites the section B
Decision validation to four clean options on every run.

## Cross-checks that ride along

- The month's locked targets are seeded into the 1.4 render sample by name
  (see [1.4.md](1.4.md)) - the pages the month depends on get checked
  deliberately.
- Deep-work rows (section C) carry: architecture decisions for the owner,
  new builds routed to the Content list, measurement rows for
  pulled-to-recrawl targets, and open verification items. Nothing routed
  silently.

## Gate

Done when: 3-5 targets sit in section A with computed baselines and
evidence-based angles (step-0 notes wherever the date gate fired), section B
lists the candidates for the content manager, section C routes every
decision and new build, section D carries exactly 3 bullets Janelle can
paste, the 1.5 flags match the lock, and the owner has signed off the
selection.
