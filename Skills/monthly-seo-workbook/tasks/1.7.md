# 1.7 Run Competitor Keyword Gap & Backlink Gap Analysis

> **Read [REASONING.md](../REASONING.md) first.** This tab's whole failure mode
> is inference dressed as analysis. The EW pilot produced three materially
> wrong briefs from "expert inference from partial evidence" before the method
> below was forced into shape. Nothing on this tab ships un-verified.

**Tab:** `1.7 Gap Analysis` · **Owner:** Roger · **Feeds:** 1.8, backlinks team
**Engine:**

```bash
python scripts/gap_collect.py serps <client>                 # phase 1
# -> judgment gate: choose 2-3 COMPARABLE competitors from the table
python scripts/gap_collect.py gaps <client> a.com,b.com      # phase 2
# -> judgment gate: cluster the verified gaps, map to existing pages
python scripts/gap_collect.py winners <client> primaries.json # phase 3
python scripts/gap_collect.py links <client> a.com,b.com <their ranking pages>
# -> author briefs from the evidence, fill via the per-client fill script
```

The phases are separated BY the judgment gates, on purpose. The engine collects;
the judgments (competitor relevance, clustering, brief writing) are made by a
person or the agent reasoning over that evidence, never by a lookup.

---

## Scope

Row 2 of the tab: last month's worked keywords + this month's 1.5 candidates.
The scoped query set is the client's GSC-derived commercial queries (the same
ones behind the 1.6 prompt set), commercial intent first, ~12 per client. Local
clients get LOCAL SERPs (Salt Lake City for the Utah four, Denver for Arsenal):
a national pull strips the market the business actually competes in.

**Relevancy outranks volume, commercial outranks informational** - owner rule.
A keyword the buyer would never search does not enter the table however big its
volume. On EW that cut the teacher-audience clusters (self-reflection,
competencies, portfolios) that a volume sort would have kept.

---

## Competitor set: comparables only, from live SERPs

Aggregate the scoped live top-10s into a domain-frequency table, then classify
every recurring domain:

- **COMPARABLE** - a same-type business that could take this client's customer.
  These are the only valid competitors and the only pages ever reverse-engineered.
- **FIXTURE** - government, state instrument owners, framework owners,
  universities, marketplaces, forums. They rank on who they are, not how their
  content is built. They contribute exactly two things: intent evidence and the
  ranking ceiling. They are never templates. EW's Danielson brief once told
  writers to imitate a 296-word state page - that is the mistake this
  classification exists to prevent.
- **Directories/publishers** - excluded from the set, but the ones ranking on
  money queries feed section B as link targets (principalcenter.com on EW).

Evidence bar: top 10 on roughly a third of scoped queries. The owner signs off
the set. AI-channel competitors (1.6) are a cross-reference, never a vote: on
EW, TeachBoost and Frontline led AI mentions and ranked in ZERO live SERPs.

---

## Keyword gaps: Labs discovers, live SERPs decide

Pull each competitor's top-10 keyword inventory from Labs (discovery ONLY - the
index is documented 8+ weeks stale per keyword), junk-filter (their brands,
customer portals, logins), drop word-order/plural variants with a sorted
word-bag, then **live-verify every survivor**: their position top 10, ours
absent/outside 10/trailing 5+. On EW, verification killed 5 of the first 13,
including a "them #10" that was #14 live and two rankings that no longer
existed. A gap row that was not live-verified does not exist.

---

## The tab is one row per KEYWORD CLUSTER

Queries in a cluster are ranked by the same competitor content item (Observation
Copilot covers all nine T-TESS variants with one page). Columns: Keyword
Cluster | Keywords (top queries with individual volumes) | **Volume = cluster
sum** (the opportunity) | **Clicks = our page's current GSC May-Jul clicks**
(the baseline at stake - it calibrates edit risk: a 5-click page can be
rewritten freely, a 292-click page gets restructured, never cut) | Intent |
Competitor | Their Ranking URL | Our Best Page | Expansion Angle | Additional
Comments (cluster total, verification trail, brief-rebuild notes).

One blank row between sections. Rows expand to fit the findings - the template
row count is layout, not a cap, but the bar is "moves the needle", not "fills
the table".

## Expansion Angle: observation, never inference

The brief is what to incorporate or redraft so the page matches the intent the
SERP demonstrably rewards. Its evidence base, all collected by the engine:

1. **Our page parsed** (headings, word count - via DFS when the site blocks us).
2. **The comparable's ranking page parsed.** Priority on comparables that
   outrank our page.
3. **The top-3 winners parsed + People Also Ask** for the cluster primary.
   Fixtures among them inform intent and ceiling only. Where winners share no
   pattern, the brief says the SERP is unresolved instead of manufacturing a
   consensus - a split SERP is an opening, not a problem.

What the EW evidence overturned, kept here as the standing warning: "depth
wins" was prescribed from a competitor ranking LAST while 227-615-word
authority pages held the top (Danielson); a "commercial" query turned out to
belong to a nature app and app stores (observation apps); a 590-volume query
was part behavioural-psychology audience (reinforcement). None of that is
knowable by inference.

**Format for the writing team**, inside the cell with line breaks:

```
HEADLINE INSTRUCTION.

WHAT THE LIVE SERP SHOWS: <observed evidence, fixtures labelled as fixtures>

DO THIS:
1. <specific action, with the PAA question or winner heading it serves>
2. ...

NOTE: <caution - current clicks at stake, volume caveats>

REALISTIC TARGET: <position range, honest about fixtures above it>
```

PAA questions are Google's own record of what askers also want - they become
the sub-heading inventory, often quoted verbatim as H2s.

## Backlink gaps: relevancy over authority - owner rule, verbatim intent

"I'd rather get a DR20 link from a highly relevant site than a DA90 generic
news link. Those are for show." Section B lists domains linking to competitors,
ranked by RELEVANCE first, authority second. On EW the relevance sort surfaced
a school district, an education purchasing co-op and educator bloggers, while
the authority sort led with radio-syndication junk.

Check page-level referring domains first: on EW every ranking page had 0-1,
proving the SERPs are won on content and there was NO backlink gap to close -
a finding, not a failure. Domain-level diff second. Verify the crown-jewel
targets by fetching them (EW: principalcenter's roundup features TeachBoost
only - explaining the whole 1.6 AI-mention anomaly). Deliberate **N** rows stay
in the table with reasons (a competitor-owned domain, an unearnable .edu) so
nobody chases them from a raw export.

## Commercial routing and click viability - owner correction, 2026-08-18

Caught across all eight first-pass tabs: the engine anchored on where
COMPETITORS rank (their blogs), so briefs inherited their content shape and the
page-mapper picked our highest-click token match (usually a blog post).
Commercial-first was applied to keyword selection but not to routing. The
corrected rules, which Snow's v2 tab is the reference implementation of:

1. **Every commercial-intent cluster routes to a commercial page** (service,
   product, collection). Blog posts are demoted to FEEDERS - internal-link
   passes into the money page - never the investment target. A TOFU explainer
   is not a commercial container however many clicks it has (Cudio's
   what-is-odoo case).
2. **Click viability gates priority.** A cluster earns its slot when the client
   shows click evidence in the territory: queries at position 4-15 earning at
   least one click, or a commercial page already earning. Pages with zero
   clicks AND no impressions are parked with a written reason, not briefed
   (Superior Millcreek/Murray).
3. **The snippet-rescue exception is kept and labelled.** Zero clicks on heavy
   impressions (Comfort's furnace page: 14k impressions, 0 clicks) means
   Google already ranks the page and the snippet loses the click - that is the
   cheapest recovery available and stays IN, explicitly labelled
   snippet-rescue.
4. **Dedicated commercial pages may be RECOMMENDED as new builds** when the
   intent is commercial, the service/product genuinely exists, and no page
   does. The existing-pages-only rule still governs informational content.
5. **Product-line flags, not briefs**, for demand competitors capture with
   products the client does not sell (Snow: trays, mouthwash) - merchandising
   decisions surfaced to the owner.
6. **Competitor sets exclude multi-category CPG/authority brands** even when
   they dominate the SERPs (Snow: Crest/Colgate excluded by owner) - they win
   on domain weight, not strategy a specialist can copy. Same fixture logic,
   applied at brand level.

**Parked-row convention (standing):** a parked cluster STAYS in section A -
cluster name suffixed `- PARKED`, `our_page` reads "None assigned - row
parked, no refresh slot", the brief states the parking reason and the
RE-ENTRY CONDITIONS, and comments carry the date + rule that parked it. A
parked row is a documented decision, same philosophy as section B's
deliberate-N rows; deleting it would just let next month's pass rediscover
and re-reject the same cluster.
