## The two lanes - never merge them

2.1 implements findings from **1.2 + 1.4** (first-party evidence: GSC, live
checks). Rahman's **1.3 + 2.2 Fix Sheet** implements SEMrush findings under
per-row owner approval. Different sources, trust levels, owners and
completion measures - the split is the org chart working.

**Dedupe DIRECTION (corrected on the EW pilot):** when both lanes find the
same issue, the fix lives in **2.1** and the 1.3 sheet's row gets
"Covered in 2.1? = **Y**". Not the reverse - the column exists on the 1.3
sheet precisely because 2.1 wins overlaps.

## Fix-row format - both owner rules, same day

1. **Brief format with real line breaks**: HEADLINE / WHAT'S WRONG /
   numbered DO / VERIFY. Never one flowing paragraph (block scalars in
   fixes.yaml carry the breaks into the cell).
2. **Every row self-contained**: every page involved named by FULL URL in
   the cell; every redirect or link action names its exact target URL;
   judgments become inline RECOMMENDED defaults with reasoning (owner
   vetoes) - never a pointer to another tab, report, or pending decision.
   The failed version said "301 this stub to the chosen canonical" with one
   URL named and the target parked in another tab - the owner said he would
   rather just download the SEMrush report, which is the whole value
   proposition inverted. If implementing a row requires looking something
   up, the row failed. (Naming a TOOL location - "the full list is in GSC >
   Pages > ..." - is an instruction, not a pointer, and is fine for lists
   too long to paste.)

Only #, Source, Fix, Where/URL are populated. **Implemented By stays blank -
assignment is the owner's** - and Verified How / Indexing Requested / Date
fill in as work completes. Priority order per the task description:
indexation blockers > redirects/links > CWV/performance.

**Rule 12 applies to every recommended target**: live-verify before naming
it. The pilot's checks caught a proposed 301 target that 404'd
(superiorwaterandair.com/services/emergency-services/) and found the REAL
structure (the live child under the dead parent) - and turned a
"restore or 301" instruction into exact, correct targets.

## The reconciliation check - the owner's favorite guard

`fill_2_1.py` ends every run by reading the 1.2 findings log and 1.4 fix
items, following each row's "Routed To" value, and verifying the item
actually appears on the target tab (2.1 or 1.8) - printing `!! MISSING`
loudly otherwise. It exists because the owner manually caught two routed
findings absent from their target tabs and said, correctly, that he should
never be the reconciliation mechanism. Zero missing is the gate; a fill that
prints MISSING is not done.

Two matching lessons: the fix cells must CONTAIN the finding's URLs (a
consolidated TTFB row must name the three measured template URLs), and a
long URL must never be wrapped across lines in the yaml (the broken path
text defeats the matcher and the reader alike).

## Sequencing judgment the queue must encode

- **Old-tail internal-link work WAITS for the 1.8 quality review** when the
  same vintage of posts sits in "Crawled - currently not indexed" (Google
  already declined them on quality). Building crawl paths to posts a review
  may prune is double work. Beehive: 82 in the quality queue, so the 71
  no-crawl-path siblings hold.
- **Stale GSC verdicts get recrawl actions, not page edits** (SameDay's 90
  stale-noindex URLs: VALIDATE FIX + URL Inspection requests on the money
  subset - the pages were verified fine).
- **SPA clients**: everything queues behind the render fix, including
  canonical-tag fixes (tags in a JS shell are themselves render-dependent).

## Safety rails (in the engine)

- `snapshot_tab()` saves the tab's current values to
  `Clients/<folder>/monthly_plan/tab-backups/` before every fill - an
  overwrite of manual edits is always recoverable. Added after a 1.8 refill
  clobbered owner edits with no way back.
- The workbook's template example row (a fake fix on every fresh 2.1 tab)
  is cleared by the fill - never leave fake data above real rows.
- All writes verified by read-back, 10pt font automatic.

## Gate

Done when: every fix row is self-contained and priority-ordered, recommended
targets are live-verified, the 1.3 sheet's overlaps carry "Covered in 2.1? =
Y", the reconcile check prints **0 missing**, and Implemented By is blank
for the owner's assignment pass.
